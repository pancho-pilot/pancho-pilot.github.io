About PANCHO

PANCHO is a Cycle 33 Hubble Space Telescope program (GO-18074) to understand the nature of the star-forming gas in the low-metallicity dwarf galaxy, IC 1613.

This site will eventually host -- or at least point to -- data and results from that program.

What's in a Name?

The name PANCHO is both a pun and an acronym. It stands for Piloting a Novel Calibration for H2 Observations and, as a pilot program, is named for Pancho Barnes, one of the pioneering female aviators of the interwar period. Our logo is inspired by the sign to her famous ranch in California's Antelope Valley.
