About PANCHO

PANCHO is a Cycle 33 HST program (GO-18074) to understand the nature of the star-forming gas in the low-metallicity dwarf galaxy, IC 1613. The name PANCHO is both a pun and an acronym. It stands for Piloting a Novel Calibration for H2 Observations and, as a pilot program, is named for Pancho Barnes, one of the famous female aviators of the interwar period.

This site will eventually host data and results from that program.
